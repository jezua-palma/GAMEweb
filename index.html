TIP Tips

  • Clear all enemies in a room to unlock doors
  • Dash has a cooldown - use it wisely!
  • Collect gold coins to boost your final score
  • Boss rooms appear every 5 floors
  • Rest rooms restore partial health
  • Event rooms can grant blessings, trader deals, or challenge waves
  • Watch Threat and Combo HUD indicators to time aggressive pushes
